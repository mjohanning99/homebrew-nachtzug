# Nachtzug for Homebrew

[Nachtzug](https://mjohanning99.github.io/nachtzug/) is a DAW (digital audio workstation) made for beginners. It lets you create electronic music, in the style of the Berlin School for example, without much prior knowledge. You don’t need to know any music theory either: Nachtzug is built so that every note automatically lands in the right scale.

## Installing

```sh
brew install mjohanning99/nachtzug/nachtzug
```

Nachtzug needs a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 15 Sequoia or newer. It isn’t signed by a registered Apple developer, so the first time, macOS says it can’t check Nachtzug for malware: click **Done**, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.

- To update Nachtzug: `brew upgrade nachtzug`
- To remove it: `brew uninstall nachtzug`
- To remove it with its settings, the spare copies of your songs and the sounds you saved in it: `brew uninstall --zap nachtzug`

The songs you’ve saved yourself stay where you put them either way.
